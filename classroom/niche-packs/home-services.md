# Niche pack — Home services (HVAC / trades / local ops)

**Buyer:** Owner-operators and office managers who lose jobs after hours.  
**Job:** Make the missed call feel expensive → show the phone answered → one clear next step (call / book).

## Objections
- “AI will mess up my customers”
- “We already have an answering service”
- “Looks fake / kids’ app”
- Price / contract fear

## Hook bank (from observed patterns + classroom cards)
1. After-hours ring into void + time stamp  
2. Geo + “missed call” overlay in first 2s  
3. Pain-stat (only with PLACEHOLDER/user proof)  
4. Real phone / van / bay — not abstract rings  
5. Sample caller line from the brand’s own site (ASSET_VERIFIED)

## Proof types that work
- Demo of answer → book/transfer (waveform or call UI)  
- Hours / overflow included (published offer facts)  
- Owner quote (USER_SUPPLIED only)  
- Avoid invented “X calls answered”

## Offers
- Book access / demo call  
- Call the published number  
- After-hours included (when true on site)

## Platform notes
- Meta/Reels: safe zones; muted-first captions  
- Google often surfaces **search_text** — do not invent 0–3s from those  
- Prefer hard cuts every ~1.5–2s for short-form DR

## Visual conventions
- Flat UI documentary for product screens  
- Real facility/van light for geo trust  
- Brand mascot: optional — demote if it reads kids-app on cold traffic (B1.2)

## Sample shot lists (timing skeletons)
See cards: `hvac-missed-call-void-01`, `local-geo-phone-02`, `voice-call-ui-demo-03`.

## Slop list
- Neon AI brain / rubber UI  
- Silent 8s hook as “the ad”  
- Caption duplicating the headline  
- Jargon CTA (“access call”) without the phone number large in safe zone  
- Slow fades, 0 hard cuts  
- Invented navy/mint when the live brand is near-black/cyan
