# YouTube Shorts

Checked: 2026-10-11

Sources:

- [YouTube Shorts ads: asset specs](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697?hl=en)
- [About video ad formats](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464)
- [About video ad specs](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13547298?hl=en)

## Sizes

- Vertical 9:16 is recommended. Horizontal and square can serve, with horizontal assets blurred top and bottom in the vertical Shorts player.
- Recommended picture: 1080×1920. Minimum HD cited on the general spec page: 720×1280 vertical.
- The creative must be a public or unlisted YouTube video.
- Up to 3 minutes is eligible. Only the first 60 seconds play in the Shorts feed. An overlay at 50 seconds can send the viewer to the watch page for the rest.
- Preferred length is under 60 seconds. Action ads: 10–30 seconds. Video reach: 6–60 seconds. Other campaign types named on the page (Video action, App, Performance Max): 10–60 seconds.

## Safe zone

The general video-spec page says overlays and buttons move by format and screen, and it points to safe-area images and PNG templates for horizontal, vertical, and square. It does not print a single pixel inset in the text. On mobile, like, comment, and share sit on the right. A CTA can appear at 3 seconds (Performance Max, App, Demand Gen) or 10 seconds (Video view and Video reach).

## Captions

Shorts ads use the channel name. Descriptions entered for the ad are limited to 90 characters; overflow past 1 line on mobile or 2 lines on desktop and TV is truncated. Google recommends a 40-character headline and a 90-character description so the mobile CTA card can show. Sound (music, voice, or both) is recommended. Text overlays are recommended so the ad still reads with the UI on top.
