Checked: 2026-10-11

Sources:

## Sizes

Skippable in-stream plays before, during, or after a video. The viewer can skip after 5 seconds. The formats page lists no maximum length and recommends under 3 minutes.

Non-skippable:

- Bumper: up to 6 seconds (see `youtube-bumper.md`).
- Standard non-skippable: 7–15 seconds.
- 30-second non-skippable is for connected TV and must be horizontal. Square or vertical 30-second assets will not run.
- The non-skippable page also says the ad should be 30 seconds or shorter.

Recommended picture: 1920×1080, 1080×1920, or 1080×1080. Minimum HD: 1280×720, 720×1280, or 480×480. The video has to be public or unlisted on YouTube. File size up to 256 GB on the general spec page.

## Safe zone

Use the safe-area templates on the video-spec page. Companion banner on desktop is 300×60, JPEG/GIF/PNG, 150 KB max. Important type and logos stay inside the template so the skip control and end cards do not cover them. The first 5 seconds of a skippable ad have to carry the point before the skip control.

## Captions

Non-skippable headline: 15 characters or fewer. If there is a CTA, 10 characters or fewer. Those limits are on the non-skippable spec page. Burned-in captions are not given a separate character cap in the text retrieved on this date.
