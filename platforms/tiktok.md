# TikTok in-feed

Checked: 2026-10-11

Source: [TikTok Auction In-Feed Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads) (page says last updated June 2026).

## Sizes

Non-Spark:

- Vertical, recommended: 9:16, at least 540×960.
- Horizontal: 16:9, at least 960×540.
- Square: 1:1, at least 640×640.
- Files: .mp4, .mov, .mpeg, .3gp, .avi. Up to 10 minutes. At most 500 MB. Bitrate at least 516 kbps.

Spark Ads (pull): .mp4 or .mov. Duration is not restricted on this page. Captions come from the organic post, up to 4 lines including emoji.

## Safe zone

TikTok does not publish one pixel inset. The page says the safe zone depends on dimension, caption length, and extra formats, and it links downloadable templates (standard LTR, LTR with anchor, Arabic RTL, RTL with anchor). Use the template that matches the ad. Profile photo, if supplied, is 98×98 with the key mark inside the center 66×66.

## Captions

Non-Spark captions are white in a font the advertiser cannot change. The page says they do not support clickable links, @ symbols, or hashtags. Account name shows one line: 20 characters, or 10 for Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
