# Instagram Reels

Checked: 2026-10-11

Sources:

- [Instagram Reels video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels)
- [Safe zones for Stories and Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490)

## Sizes

- 9:16, recommended 1440×2560.
- MP4 or MOV. H.264, square pixels, fixed frame rate, progressive scan, stereo AAC at 128 kbps or higher.
- Duration: 0 seconds to 15 minutes. File size up to 4 GB.
- Minimum width: 250 px under 30 seconds; 500 px at 30 seconds or longer.

## Safe zone

Leave at least 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom, and 6% of each side free of text, logos, and other key elements. The safe-zone help page says 9:16 ads in Instagram Feed use the same guidance, and a Reels disclaimer should leave the bottom 40% clear. Ads Manager can show the zone as a yellow guardrail. Taller-than-9:16 screens may zoom and crop outside the zone, or letterbox with black.

## Captions

Captions are optional and recommended. Sound is optional and strongly recommended. Primary text recommendation: 44 characters. The ads guide says not to use licensed music; it points advertisers to original audio or Meta Sound Collection.
