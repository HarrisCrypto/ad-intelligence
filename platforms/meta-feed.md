# Meta feed

Checked: 2026-10-11

Source: [Facebook Feed video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/facebook-feed). Safe-zone note from [Meta safe zones](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490).

## Sizes

- Recommended ratio 4:5 at 1440×1800.
- MP4, MOV, or GIF. H.264, square pixels, fixed frame rate, progressive scan, stereo AAC at 128 kbps or higher.
- Duration: 1 second to 241 minutes. File size up to 4 GB.
- Minimum width and height: 120 px.

The Feed page notes that some awareness and reach objectives hide the headline, description, and CTA footer on Facebook Feed for mobile.

## Safe zone

The safe-zone help page says Instagram Feed ads that are not 9:16 (including 1:1 and 4:5) should keep the bottom and side edges free of text, logos, and key elements. It does not publish a percentage for 4:5. 9:16 video in Instagram Feed uses the Reels and Stories zone (see `instagram-reels.md`). Do not invent a pixel inset for 4:5.

## Captions

Captions are optional and recommended. Sound is optional and recommended. Primary text: 50–150 characters. Headline: 27 characters.
