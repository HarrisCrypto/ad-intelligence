# Meta Stories

Checked: 2026-10-11

Sources:

- [Instagram Stories video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-story)
- [Facebook Stories video ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/facebook-story)
- [Safe zones](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490)

Instagram and Facebook Stories do not share one duration or one bottom inset. Use the placement you are actually buying.

## Instagram Stories

- 9:16, recommended 1440×2560. MP4, MOV, or GIF. H.264, stereo AAC at 128 kbps or higher.
- Upload duration: 1 second to 60 minutes. File size up to 4 GB. Minimum width 250 px. Aspect tolerance 1%.
- Ads under 16 seconds play in full. At 16 seconds or longer, Instagram may split the ad into 1, 2, or 3 cards. Some goals offer "Keep watching".
- Safe zone on this page: roughly 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom, and 6% of each side.
- Primary text: 125 characters. Captions and sound are optional and recommended.

## Facebook Stories

- Same file type, 9:16, and 1440×2560 recommendation.
- Upload duration: 1 second to 3 minutes. File size up to 4 GB. Minimum width 250 px.
- Ads of 15 seconds or less play in full. Longer ads split into 1, 2, or 3 cards, then "Play full".
- Safe zone on this page: roughly 14% (250 px) of the top and 20% (340 px) of the bottom free of text and logos. This page does not state a side percentage.
- Primary text: 125 characters. Headline: 40 characters.

Reels disclaimers are a separate rule: leave the bottom 40% clear. That line is on the safe-zone help page, not on the Facebook Stories placement page.
