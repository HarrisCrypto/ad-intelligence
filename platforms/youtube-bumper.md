# YouTube bumper

Checked: 2026-10-11

Sources:

- [About video ad formats](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375464)
- [Non-skippable in-stream ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11462260?hl=en)

## Sizes

A bumper is a non-skippable in-stream ad of 5 to 6 seconds on the formats page ("6 seconds, 5 seconds minimum"). The non-skippable page says a video of 6 seconds or less runs as a bumper. It plays before, during, or after another video on YouTube and, when opted in, Google video partners. Picture recommendations match other YouTube video ads: 1920×1080, 1080×1920, or 1080×1080, hosted as a public or unlisted YouTube video.

## Safe zone

Same universal YouTube safe-area templates as in-stream. There is no skip button, so the frame can use the full 6 seconds, but end cards and companion UI can still cover edges. Desktop companion banner: 300×60, 150 KB max.

## Captions

One line of type is the practical caption, because the ad is only 5–6 seconds. The non-skippable spec lists a headline of 15 characters or fewer and a CTA of 10 characters or fewer. Do not stack a second message.
