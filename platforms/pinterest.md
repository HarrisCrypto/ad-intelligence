# Pinterest

Checked: 2026-10-11

Sources:

- [Review ad specs](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs)
- [Review Pin specs](https://help.pinterest.com/en/article/review-pin-specs) (organic video safe zone)

## Sizes

Standard-width video ads:

- .mp4, .mov, or .m4v. H.264 or H.265. Up to 2 GB.
- Length: 4 seconds to 15 minutes. Recommended 6–15 seconds.
- Aspect: shorter than 1:2 and taller than 1.91:1. Recommended square 1:1 or vertical 2:3, 4:5, or 9:16.

Max-width video ads are paid only, mobile feed, and cannot be taller than 1:1. Same file types, size, and length.

Organic video Pin specs on the Pin page differ: 4 seconds to 5 minutes, full-bleed recommended at 9:16 or 1080×1920, with other options 1:2, 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, or 1:1.

## Safe zone

The ad spec page does not print a pixel safe zone for standard video ads. The Pin spec page, for video Pins, says to keep elements inside top 270 px, left 65 px, right 195 px, and bottom 790 px. Idea ads on the ad spec page use a different box: top 270, left 65, right 195, bottom 440, on a 1080×1920 full-bleed recommendation. Do not mix those insets across formats.

White or near-white image backgrounds get a 4% black veil in light mode. The ad spec page says this does not apply to videos.

## Captions

Title: 100 characters. About the first 40 show in feed (30 for double-byte languages). Description: up to 800 characters. Descriptions do not show in home or search feed, and do not show for ads viewed up close. They are used for relevance. Video ads play muted in feed; sound is available after the viewer taps, per the video-ads help linked from the spec page.
